FAQ

How is the cost calculated?

For each vendor we apply its published list price to your inputs: per route for GorillaDesk, by active-customer tier for Pocomos, and the cheapest plan with enough users (plus published extra-user fees) for Jobber, Housecall Pro and QuoteIQ. Taxes, payment processing, SMS, add-ons and intro promotions aren’t included.

Why does the cheapest line show a different plan for each vendor?

The calculator picks the cheapest plan that fits your headcount, but plans differ in features. Recurring billing and route optimization, for example, are on higher plans at some vendors. Check the plan’s features on the vendor’s pricing page.

Why aren’t PestPac, FieldRoutes and Briostack calculated?

They don’t publish prices. They’re listed so you remember to ask for a written quote.

Who counts as a user?

Everyone who logs in: technicians plus office staff. GorillaDesk only charges for technicians who run their own route, and Pocomos doesn’t charge per user.
