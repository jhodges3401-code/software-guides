What to check before you choose

Does the report match your AHJ?

Fire marshals may adopt a specific NFPA 96 edition. Ask the vendor which edition its report follows and whether you can add local fields and state license numbers.

Reports only, or the whole business?

Report apps (HoodCleaningReport, Stampario, HoodStamp) sit alongside whatever you invoice with. All-in-ones (MyHoodClean, Smart Service, HoodOps) replace your scheduling and billing too.

Report limits

HoodCleaningReport caps reports per month by plan and sells packs, and Stampario’s free plan stops at 5 reports a month. Count your reports in a busy month before you choose.

FAQ

Is there free hood cleaning report software?

Yes. Stampario has a free plan for 5 reports a month, HoodStamp lists a free plan for 3 certificates a month, and HoodCleaningReport has a free trial of 3 reports or 3 days, per their sites checked October 17, 2026.

What’s the cheapest all-in-one hood cleaning app?

Of the hood-specific apps that publish prices, MyHoodClean is $49.99 a month ($34.99 a month billed yearly) and covers reports, scheduling, invoicing and a website. Stampario Pro is cheaper at $29 a month but focuses on reports.

Do these reports guarantee NFPA 96 compliance?

No software can. Compliance depends on the work done and on your local Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ), which may adopt a specific NFPA 96 edition or add its own requirements. Check with your fire marshal.
